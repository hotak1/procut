ProCut Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

ProCut is a photo and video editor. Your privacy matters, so the app is built to work on your device.

Data we collect

None. ProCut does not collect, store or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

Photos and videos

Photos and videos you choose with the system picker are edited only on your iPhone or iPad. They are never uploaded to any ProCut server. AI cut-out and 3D motion use Apple Vision on your device. When you export, ProCut asks for permission to add the edited file to your Photos library; it does not read the rest of your library.

Auto captions

When you choose to generate captions, ProCut uses Apple's Speech Recognition framework with your permission. Where your device supports it, recognition runs entirely on the device. For some languages, Apple may process the audio on its servers to return the text; this is handled by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy. ProCut never receives or stores your audio or captions on any server.

Purchases

The optional ProCut Premium unlock is processed by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details.

Children

ProCut does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.
