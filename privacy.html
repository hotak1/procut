ProCut Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

ProCut is a photo and video editor. Your privacy matters, so the app is built to work entirely on your device.

Data we collect

None. ProCut does not collect, store or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

Photos and videos

Photos and videos you choose with the system picker are edited only on your iPhone. They are never uploaded to any server. When you export, ProCut asks for permission to add the edited file to your Photos library; it does not read the rest of your library.

Purchases

The optional ProCut Premium unlock is processed by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details.

Children

ProCut does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.
